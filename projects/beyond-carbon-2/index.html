Reducing Aviation Emissions

Concrete strategies for cutting emissions from air travel.

Lecture 2 of the lecture series Beyond Carbon: Debating the Future, University of Zurich, spring semester 2026

How Can Air Travel Emissions Be Reduced Most Effectively?

Event description

The panel discussed concrete strategies for reducing emissions from air travel, one of the largest sources of emissions for universities and many other organisations.

Speakers

  • Prof. Katharina Michaelowa, University of Zurich
  • Linde Warland, University of Zurich
  • Prof. Andreas I. Mueller, University of Zurich

Moderation

  • Prof. Bjarne Steffen, ETH Zurich

Speaker profiles and further details are available in the review of this lecture on the UZH website.
