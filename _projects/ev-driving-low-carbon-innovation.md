long_description: Climate-related frontier innovation, measured by patent filings, has been declining since 2012, while deployment of existing technologies is rising. The presentation argued that making carbon-free technologies fully cost-competitive with high-carbon alternatives should be a primary objective of climate policy, with science, technology, innovation and industrial policies as key levers, complemented by carbon pricing and the removal of fossil fuel subsidies. Part of a full-day workshop on green industrial policy with the Hertie School, the OECD and the Berlin School of Economics.
