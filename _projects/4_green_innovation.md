An in-person conference in Berlin with three panels on how policy can foster innovation for the green transition. Opening remarks by Cornelia Woll, President of the Hertie School.

The first panel looked at carbon contracts for difference as a new policy tool, with Karsten Neuhoff (DIW), Friedrich von Schönfeld (BMWK) and Johanna Schiele (European Commission). The second panel discussed the challenges of decarbonizing the cement industry, with Carolin Boßmeyer (Heidelberg Materials) and Volker Thome (Fraunhofer IPA). The third panel, moderated by Nils Handler, focused on carbon removal technologies, with Marian Krüger (remove), Lisa Mangertsreder (Verein für Negativemissionen) and Devina Banerjee (Carbon Gap).
