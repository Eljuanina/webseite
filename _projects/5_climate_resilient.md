Adaptation to climate change is becoming ever more urgent, especially in the Global South. This webinar asked how climate-sensitive macroeconomic modelling can help understand the economic impacts of climate change, avoid maladaptation, and how such tools can be made available to developing and emerging economies.

Keynotes by Ulrich Volz (SOAS, University of London) and Iryna Payosova (World Resources Institute), with discussants Christoph Schult (IWH Halle) and Florent John McIsaac (World Bank).

Organized in collaboration with the GIZ global project on Policy Advice for Climate-Resilient Economic Development (CRED).
