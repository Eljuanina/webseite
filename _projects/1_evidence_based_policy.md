How can governments design better policies through experimental evidence, and how can Germany and the EU build a culture of policy learning? This one-day workshop brought together economists, policymakers and practitioners to discuss how randomized controlled trials and quasi-experimental methods can improve the design and evaluation of public policy, with a focus on climate, innovation and healthcare.

The workshop featured sessions on experimental innovation policy, evidence-based healthcare and science policy, and how German ministries put evidence-based practices into action. Welcome and closing remarks by Uwe Cantner, Ariel Dora Stern and Nils Handler.

Organized by the [d\carb future economy forum](https://www.decarb.world/), with support from the Hertie School Centre for Sustainability and the Berlin School of Economics.
