Co-organized a **[Policy Roundtable on Harnessing Rigorous Research to Shape Climate Policy](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/event/policy-roundtable-harnessing-rigorous-research-shape-climate-policy)** with J-PAL Europe and the IZA Institute of Labor Economics in Bonn, Germany (14 May 2025). The event brought together senior representatives from policy organizations across Europe to discuss how rigorous evidence can shape climate policy, with speakers from PIK, GIZ, the German Council on Foreign Relations, and the Centre for Net Zero.
