## Course Overview

Organizations that want to decarbonize can choose between many different measures, and these need to be tested to make sure that theoretical emission reductions turn into actual ones. This course guides students through deploying a randomized controlled trial (RCT) in a university setting, starting with an introduction to the sustainability measures already in place at UZH.

Students learn what makes a good RCT and the intuition and mathematics behind it, how to develop RCT ideas or identify the need for one through a literature review, and how to critique existing designs. Each session combines a lecture with a discussion on applying the topic to the students' own projects.

## Projects and Grading

Students choose between an **Experiment Development Project**, in which they write a pre-analysis plan for an RCT, and a **Literature Review Project**, in which they identify gaps where future RCTs on sustainability should be conducted. Projects can be done in groups of one to three students.

- Final project: 80%
- In-class participation: 20%
